<product_requirements>
The KineTrack application is designed as an AI-powered tool for physiotherapists to automate clinical processes and enhance patient follow-up.

**Core Problem:** Automate repetitive administrative tasks, streamline patient monitoring, and offer personalized treatment programs.

**Initial Core Features:**
1.  **Patient Management:** Capture patient name, age, pathology, medical prescription, anamnèse (questionnaire with checkboxes/free text), and bilan results (tests, scores, mobility, pain).
2.  **AI Document Generation:** Generate structured anamnèse reports, convert bilan results into syntheses, propose personalized treatment plans (exercises, frequency, progression), and create doctor letters.
3.  **Communication & Archiving:** Automate sending exercise programs (with video links, follow-up calendar) to patients and archive patient files (PDF/text).
4.  **Reporting (Optional initially):** Generate progression graphs for patients and therapists.
5.  **User Interface:** Secure homepage, dedicated tabs for Nouveau patient, Bilan, Exercices, Suivi, and an Exporter button.

**Implemented Enhancements based on User Feedback:**
1.  **Patient Deletion:** Ability to delete patient records.
2.  **Extended Exercise Database:** Increased number of exercises (24) covering more body zones (10).
3.  **Interactive Body Schema:** Visual exercise filtering by body zone.
4.  **Patient Data Automation:** Automatic capitalization for patient names/surnames.
5.  **AI Document Editor:** Interface to modify AI-generated documents with specific AI instructions.
6.  **Advanced Anamnesis:** Integrated Pain Drawing component, Autres (Other) text field for general activities, and Red/Yellow/Blue Flags.
7.  **Suggestions System:** A collaborative tab for user feedback submission and status tracking.
8.  **Messaging & Notifications:** A social-media style communication system for patient/therapist interactions and activity notifications.
9.  **Advanced Programming System:** Multi-phase programs, automatic load/volume calculations, pain tracking, automatic adaptation with manual control.

**Currently in Progress/Pending:**
1.  **Coaching Avancé:** Planning macro/micro/meso cycles, personalized coaching assistant (adapting programs based on sport, leisure, etc.).
2.  **Agenda & Planning:** Therapist agenda view, integrated patient planning/calendar with upcoming and planned sessions.
</product_requirements>
